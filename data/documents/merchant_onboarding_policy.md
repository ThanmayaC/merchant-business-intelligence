# Merchant Onboarding Policy


**Document type:** Internal training policy\
**Version:** 1.2\
**Effective date:** 1 October 2026\

## 1. Purpose

This document describes a sample onboarding workflow for merchants
joining a fictional payment platform.

## 2. Required information

A merchant onboarding request should include the legal business name,
merchant category, registered business address, settlement account
details, an authorised contact, and the platform's required verification
documents.

## 3. Review stages

The onboarding team checks that mandatory fields are present, verifies
submitted documents, reviews the merchant category, and records an
approval or rejection decision. Incomplete applications should be
returned with a clear list of missing information.

## 4. Activation

A merchant should be marked active only after the required checks are
completed and an approval decision is recorded. The activation timestamp
and reviewer identifier should be retained for audit purposes.

## 5. Changes and escalation

Changes to settlement details require a fresh verification step.
Suspected duplicate applications or inconsistent business information
should be escalated to the operations review queue.

## 6. Data handling

Access to merchant records should be limited to authorised staff.
Sensitive account information should not be exposed in general analytics
responses.
